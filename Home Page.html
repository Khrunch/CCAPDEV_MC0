About TerryPick

TerryPick is a simple booking and discovery prototype for pickleball courts. You can browse sample court listings, check basic details/availability, and jump into a demo reservation flow.

Built as a course project to demonstrate UI pages, navigation, and simple “logged in” behavior (demo auth).
